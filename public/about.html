SOME OTHER LIFE / OUR STORY

Some other life.

An independent clothing label founded and owned by Bayley McRae. Established in 2026.

01 / THE IDEA

Give your own life a few hours too.

SOME OTHER LIFE began with Bayley's interest in the distance between the life we know and the one we imagine. Fashion became a way to give that idea a physical form — through pieces with their own identity, rather than simply following what was already being made.

The name is an invitation to look beyond the familiar. It leaves room for the individual wearing the garment to give it a meaning of their own.

02 / THE FOUNDER

Bayley McRae.

Founder / Owner / Creative Direction

Bayley develops the label's visual identity, garment concepts, insignia, digital experience and creative direction. From the first design studies to the development of the collection, the intention is to build an independent body of work with a recognisable point of view.

He is also the creator of SET ON YOU, an independent nightlife and events brand. Both projects reflect his interests in music, design and culture, while remaining separate creative identities.

03 / COLLECTION 01

BLACK SWAN.

The first collection explores the unexpected. The Insignia Long Sleeve introduces the label's visual language through contrasting shoulder panels and distressed detailing. ELSEWHERE extends that exploration into denim.

The collection is in development. Its release marks the beginning of the label, not the conclusion of its story.
