# Some Other Life — design rationale

The current direction is warm stone, bronze and ivory, matched to the doorway photograph selected by the owner. Copy is concise and collection-led. The supplied emblem is used unchanged. Small detail studies hint at Collection 01; complete garments remain unrevealed.

## Primary sources reviewed, 17 November 2026

- [Louis Vuitton Australia](https://au.louisvuitton.com/eng-au/homepage): collection names, image-led sections and brief discovery actions.
- [Louis Vuitton x Murakami](https://au.louisvuitton.com/eng-au/stories/louisvuittonxmurakami): a recognisable motif and selective detail introduce a collection's identity.
- [LOEWE SS27 precollection](https://www.loewe.com/apc/en/pd/stories-collection/ss27-precollection-campaign.html): brief, concrete descriptions of material, proportion and craft, followed by simple collection links.
- [Bottega Veneta Australia](https://www.bottegaveneta.com/en-au): short collection introductions focused on texture, material and signature detailing.
- [Drake Related](https://drakerelated.com/rooms/front): explorable spaces inform the site's interactive doorway and three subtle scene states.

## Application

Use Collection 01, First details, Texture, Embroidery and Notify me as the main labels. Remove the previous motivational paragraphs and repeated descriptions of secrecy. Let the photography and emblem carry the identity.

The original logo photograph is used directly. The site's new detail images are crops of the supplied waffle fabric and embroidered contrast panel, with no complete garment silhouette, shoulder placement, back view or blueprint. The garment asset allowlist prevents accidentally shipping an original underneath a crop or blur.

No invented production claims, handmade claims, false inventory, sales totals, discounts or release date are included. These are labelled design studies. Subscriber records are private; customer email delivery is not automatic.
